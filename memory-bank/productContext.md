# Product Context: AFIDA E-commerce Platform

## Purpose
AFIDA is an e-commerce platform designed to streamline the process of ordering products online with customized pricing for different customers. The platform serves as a bridge between suppliers and customers, allowing for efficient product browsing, ordering, and management.

## Problems Solved

### For Customers
- **Personalized Pricing**: Each customer sees their own negotiated prices for products
- **Efficient Ordering**: Streamlined shopping cart and checkout process
- **Address Management**: Save and reuse shipping and billing addresses
- **Order History**: Track past orders and their status
- **Simplified Reordering**: Easily place repeat orders

### For Administrators
- **Customer Management**: Invite and manage customer accounts with custom price lists
- **Product Management**: Easily add, update, and categorize products
- **Order Processing**: Track and update order status
- **Custom Pricing**: Set different prices for different customers
- **Inventory Visibility**: Manage product availability

## User Experience Goals

### Customer Experience
1. **Intuitive Navigation**: Easy browsing of products by category
2. **Personalized Interface**: Display of customer-specific pricing
3. **Streamlined Checkout**: Simple process for completing orders
4. **Address Management**: Easy saving and selection of addresses
5. **Order Tracking**: Clear visibility into order status

### Administrator Experience
1. **Comprehensive Dashboard**: Overview of orders, customers, and products
2. **Efficient User Management**: Simple process for adding and managing users
3. **Flexible Product Management**: Easy addition and categorization of products
4. **Price List Management**: Intuitive interface for setting customer-specific prices
5. **Order Processing**: Clear workflow for handling orders

## Key Workflows

### Customer Workflows
1. **Product Browsing**: Navigate categories and view products with personalized pricing
2. **Cart Management**: Add products, adjust quantities, and review cart
3. **Checkout Process**: Select addresses, payment method, and complete order
4. **Account Management**: Update profile and manage saved addresses
5. **Order History**: View past orders and their details

### Administrator Workflows
1. **User Management**: Create, invite, and manage customer accounts
2. **Product Administration**: Add, update, and categorize products
3. **Price List Configuration**: Set and manage customer-specific pricing
4. **Order Processing**: View, update status, and manage orders
5. **System Configuration**: Manage categories and other system settings

## Integration Points
- **Payment Processing**: Integration with payment methods (invoice, bank transfer, credit card)
- **Address Validation**: Postcode lookup for address validation
- **Email Notifications**: Order confirmations and status updates
- **Image Storage**: Product image management via Active Storage

## Success Metrics
- **Order Volume**: Number of orders processed
- **User Adoption**: Number of active customers
- **Order Completion Rate**: Percentage of carts converted to orders
- **Administrative Efficiency**: Time spent on order processing and management
- **Customer Satisfaction**: Ease of use and reliability of the platform
